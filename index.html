Privacy Policy

App: ArotLink (package: arotlink.com)
Last updated: October 6, 2026

ArotLink ("we", "our", "us") provides a mobile app that connects customers and retailers for placing and managing orders. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and account type (customer or retailer) that you provide when registering.
  • Business/delivery details: shop or delivery address and related details you enter in the app.
  • Order information: items ordered, order history, and amounts.
  • Photos: images you choose to upload (for example a profile or shop picture). We access only the images you select; we do not browse your gallery.
  • Local app data: preferences and login session stored on your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To process, display, and fulfil orders between customers and retailers.
  • To provide support and communicate about your orders or account.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud, and improve the app.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Order and contact details are shared between the customer and retailer involved in an order as needed to complete it. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety. We use service providers (such as hosting) who process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.

4. Data security

Data is transmitted to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time:

  1. Email sohel592@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include your full name, the mobile number you registered with, and your account type (customer or retailer).
  3. We may call or text that mobile number to confirm the request really comes from the account owner.
  4. Once confirmed, we delete your account and data within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted: your name, mobile number, email, address, shop details, photos, cart, and login sessions.
What may be kept: records of past orders, only where the law requires us to keep them (for example for accounting or tax), and only for as long as required.

Full instructions: Delete your ArotLink account.

6. Children's privacy

ArotLink is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: required to communicate with our servers.
  • Photos/Camera (optional): only when you choose to upload an image.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date revised.

9. Contact us

Questions or deletion requests: sohel592@gmail.com
